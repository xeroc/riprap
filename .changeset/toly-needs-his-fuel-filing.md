Add the per-pool filing framework's second pack: a payout-request wizard for the Toly Needs His Fuel bounty, keyed by its new `claimFlow` directory field like the Blade Pool's. The pack carries the bounty terms' own proof set — five proofs: ticket, ID, request evidence, handover evidence, and Toly's confirming post as a pasted URL (no declaration slot: the wallet signature on the claim is the member's statement of facts). URL slots are a new flow-pack capability (`DocSlot.url`): the member pastes the post's link and the wizard validates it against x.com/twitter.com; the manifest carries the RAW link under the slot's key (`confirmation-url: "https://…"` + sha256 of its UTF-8 bytes) — a URL has no file, so nothing is PUT for it; the manifest itself is its delivery, and the publish rows mark it delivered with the manifest POST. The serializer accepts the pool's slots (strings still mean plain file paths, so the Blade Pool's manifests stay byte-identical); the §3 qualifying-delivery self-screen and §4 exclusions line come from the terms; no emergency banner, since a bounty has no urgent peril. The wizard's shared gate states now name the routed pool and link its own detail route instead of the Blade Pool's hardcoded ones, so a Toly deep link reads honestly (not-member and not-live states included).
