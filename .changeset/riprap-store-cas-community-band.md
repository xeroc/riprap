Community pools in the directory: the mutuals store resolves unknown scan
hits through their anchored def — seed + policyHash off the account, the
derived domain_ref, the evidence daemon's CAS, a client-side
sha256-vs-policyHash check, and the riprap-def/v1 parse. A valid def yields
a derived listing in a community band below the founder list (no badges,
founder order untouched); 404, a hash mismatch, or a non-def doc drops the
pool — a state, never fallback content. The CAS read shares usePolicyDoc's
react-query key, so the directory, the generic pool page, the wizard, and
the juror session read one immutable-bytes cache per ref.
