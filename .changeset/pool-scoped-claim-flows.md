Pool-scoped claim filing and a joined-pools dashboard. The payout-request wizard is pool-scoped at `#/app/file-claim/<pool>` — driven by per-pool `ClaimFlow` packs (`src/app/file-claim/flows/`) keyed by a new `claimFlow` field on the mutuals directory (`src/mutuals/data.ts`); the Blade Pool's pack carries its policy §3/§4/§7 copy verbatim, and pack tests pin the slot paths to the manifest's canonical list and the adjudication policy pack. The `#/app` member surface is a pure chain scan behind an app hero band (backdrop on — "The app.", managing the mutuals this wallet participates in): the mutuals store finds every live pool and one membership read per pool lists the entered ones — a joined-pools table band (the /mutuals structure with the shared badge rail; name + Covered stamp, tagline, per-row button actions into that pool's wizard `#/app/file-claim/<pool>` and jury board `#/app/adjudicate/<pool>`, new pool-scoped board route) and a claims band (this wallet's filed payout requests, one block per joined pool under its name). The bare wizard route and the static-map fallbacks are gone: `resolvePoolAddress` (env override, else the pinned pubkey) resolves every pool, `useMutual`/`useMembership`/`useClaimPreflight` take an address, and `poolTiers` takes the listing's tier names. Drafts stay keyed per mutual; a legacy draft's screen record still loads.
