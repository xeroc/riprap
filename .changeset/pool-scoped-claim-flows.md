Pool-scoped claim filing, a joined-pools dashboard, and per-pool heroes. The payout-request wizard is pool-scoped at `#/app/file-claim/<pool>` — driven by per-pool `ClaimFlow` packs (`src/app/file-claim/flows/`) keyed by a new `claimFlow` field on the mutuals directory (`src/mutuals/data.ts`); the Blade Pool's pack carries its policy copy verbatim and pack tests pin slot-path parity with the manifest and the adjudication policy. The `#/app` member surface is a pure chain scan behind an app hero band (backdrop on — "The app.", managing the mutuals this wallet participates in): a joined-pools table band (the /mutuals structure with the shared badge rail; name + Covered stamp, per-row button actions into that pool's wizard `#/app/file-claim/<pool>` and jury board `#/app/adjudicate/<pool>`) and a claims band (this wallet's filed payout requests, one block per pool). Every pool page now carries its own hero component — per-pool JSX and copy under `mutuals/pages/*Hero.tsx` (founder call: heroes customize too much for a template; the Blade Pool keeps its own, slider and copy verbatim, in `pool/sections/PoolHero.tsx`), sharing only the join machine (`usePoolJoin` — reads, one-tx chip-in, covered-moment gate) and small chrome bits. The single-tier draft heroes render no tier picker: a fixed-fee chip-in CTA, a per-pool description, odds table, and the covered moment with a per-pool share message and pool-scoped juror link; honest not-live/{{PARAM}} states until the pool deploys. Pool resolution is pins-only now: the `VITE_DEVNET_MUTUAL`/`VITE_MAINNET_MUTUAL` overrides are gone — a listing's pinned pubkey is tried on whatever cluster is active (absence is chain truth, not unset env), route ids carry the pubkey (`#/m/<pin>` picks the per-cluster blade listing), and the store's scan discovers everything else. `VITE_LOCALNET_MUTUAL` stays for the Surfpool dev lane; `useMutual`/`useJoinContext` take null = unbound, never the static map. The bare wizard route and the static-map fallbacks are gone; `useMutual`/`useMembership`/`useClaimPreflight`/`useJoinContext` take an address, and `poolTiers` takes the listing's tier names. Drafts stay keyed per mutual; a legacy draft's screen record still loads. Hero copy for the eight pools (headlines, sublines, odds rows, share texts) is authored-not-doc'd — fold into the next copy-doc pass.
