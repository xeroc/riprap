Claim restore surface: a PENDING claim row on /app now opens the read-only replay of its filed claim at `#/app/file-claim/:pool/restore/:nonce`. Entry reads the evidence operator's public manifest endpoint first — a held manifest verifies against the on-chain `Dispute.evidence_hashes[0]` and lands straight on the as-filed review (facts, full-length manifest sha, dispute chip, per-slot hashes); when the operator holds nothing readable, the kept manifest.yaml uploads manually through the same chain gate — an unparseable stored or uploaded manifest falls back to the upload prompt carrying the parse message, never a dead end. Re-delivery re-attaches the document bytes (the manifest carries hashes only) and runs the publish table — manifest row first, phase spinners, per-row retry; same-hash re-deliveries are 201 no-ops and a divergent file 409s naturally. No signing and no sending anywhere in the flow — the dispute already exists on-chain. The riprap-claim/v1 parser now validates entries against the pool's own flow slots (url entries under their manifest key included) instead of a fixed five-path list — bounty manifests restore and parse; the juror package gate keeps the Blade default until its policy slots are threaded.
